I am currently a remote research assistant with Prof. Yang Lu at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, working on representation learning for biomedical data. From January to August 2027, I will join her group in person as a Research Intern in the Department of Biomedical Engineering. I also collaborate with Prof. Xiaofeng Yang at the University of Chicago on reliable medical AI.

My research interests include mathematical modeling and machine learning, with applications in medical imaging and biomedical data analysis. My current work focuses on representation learning for high-dimensional data, particularly relationships between samples and features.

I am seeking PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027. Please feel free to contact me if you think my background could be a good fit for your group.
